The MesoCarnivores data (bobcat, coyote and red fox detections at 1,437 camera-trap
sites, with site covariates) are those of Rota et al. (2016), *Methods in Ecology and
Evolution* 7:1164–1173, as distributed with the R package
[unmarked](https://cran.r-project.org/package=unmarked) (`data(MesoCarnivores)`).
Exported with `write.csv`; `NA` is an unsurveyed visit.
